COST Open Call OC-2026-1 (new COST Actions) [single-source]

  • Funder: COST Association
  • Scheme: OC-2026-1
  • Deadline: Wednesday 28 October 2026, 12:00 CET (11:00 UK)
  • Eligibility: GO — the COST page says participants can apply at any stage of their career, and the UK is one of the 41 COST Full Members. A network of proposers from several COST countries is needed (last week’s run noted a minimum of seven; not re-checked today).
  • Fit: Partial. Networking money only (about EUR 150k in year 1, then EUR 180k a year for three years); it would suit a COST Action on SAI, volcanic, wildfire or nuclear-war aerosol modelling.
  • Why it might not fit: Pays meetings, short visits and training schools only, with no research salary, and the network has to be assembled in nine days.

https://www.cost.eu/funding/open-call-a-simple-one-step-application-process/

  • Deadline: Phase 1: Monday 2 November 2026; Phase 2: 18 January 2027 (time of day not stated; one USD 500k pool for 5–10 awards)
