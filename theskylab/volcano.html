How it works

Sulphur dioxide turns into sulphate droplets that reflect sunlight for a year or two. Higher injections last longer, tropical veils spread to both hemispheres, and bigger eruptions cool less per tonne. Pinatubo in 1991 put about 15 to 20 Tg into the stratosphere and cooled the planet by about 0.4 to 0.5 °C.
