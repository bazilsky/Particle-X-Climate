Solar Radiation Management (SRM) research programme [single-source]

  • Funder: Simons Foundation (Mathematics and Physical Sciences)
  • Deadline: 6 October 2026, 12:00 noon ET (LOI); full proposal by invitation 17 February 2027
  • Eligibility: NO-GO as Applicant PI — call requires tenured/tenure-track (or equivalent) in a PhD-granting department. Named modeller on a faculty-led LOI is a different role.

  • Why it might not fit: Very short runway to the LOI; lab/modelling only, no in-situ release.

https://www.simonsfoundation.org/grant/solar-radiation-management/

ERC Starting Grant 2027 [single-source]

  • Funder: European Research Council (Horizon Europe)
  • Scheme: ERC-2027-STG
  • Deadline: 14 October 2026
  • Eligibility: GO — call allows a single PI with a UK (Associated Country) host; PhD Dec 2022 is inside the 0–10 year window. A host letter is still required to submit.
  • Fit: Bottom-up route for SAI microphysics, UKESM/UKCA/GLOMAP, atmospheric composition, or nuclear-war climate effects.
  • Why it might not fit: High excellence bar; need host commitment in time.

https://erc.europa.eu/apply-grant/starting-grant
